Insurance in plain language

Insurance is a system for sharing risk. People pay into it so one person's medical cost does not fall only on that one person.

1
People join a plan
They pay premiums to keep coverage active.
2
Care is received
The provider documents services and submits a claim.
3
The payer processes the claim
This is adjudication.
4
The EOB explains the result
It explains processing. It is not the bill itself.

Front-desk judgment case

What staff should understand

Do not confuse EOB with a bill

B
Allowed amount matters

C
Patient responsibility has logic

D
